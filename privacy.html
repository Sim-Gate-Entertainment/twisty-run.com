Website privacy

This page describes twisty-run.com. For the mobile game's data practices, see the game privacy policy.

Optional analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics”, this website loads Google Analytics to measure page visits, gameplay-trailer plays and clicks on the Google Play and App Store buttons. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish visits and receives information such as browser, device and approximate location information derived from your connection. We do not send a name, email address, game account ID or advertising ID from the website.

We remove query strings from the page address sent by our analytics configuration and send only the referring website's origin. Google advertising signals and advertising personalization are disabled. Analytics does not load before you allow it. Declining analytics does not prevent downloads or use of the website.

Your analytics choice is saved in this browser's local storage for up to 180 days. You can reopen “Analytics choices” below to change it. Withdrawing permission stops further analytics events on the page and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase data already processed by Google. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Gameplay trailer

The trailer is not loaded automatically. Choosing to play it loads a YouTube privacy-enhanced player from youtube-nocookie.com. YouTube then receives connection and playback information. You can also choose the separate YouTube link to watch on its website. See Google's privacy policy.

Store links and hosting

Download buttons take you to Google Play or the App Store, where the store's privacy practices apply. The Google Play link includes a fixed website referral campaign label; it contains no visitor identifier. The hosting provider may process connection information to serve the website.
